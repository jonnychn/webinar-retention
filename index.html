Retention Data Generator with Chart Visualization

How to Use:
1. Copy your data directly from your spreadsheet (Google Sheets, Excel, etc.), including the header row (Person, Time Duration).
2. Paste into the box below. (Tab or comma-delimited both work.)
3. Click “Process CSV Data” to generate the retention table and chart.
4. Use the download buttons to save the processed CSV and the chart image.

Example CSV Data:
Person,Time Duration
