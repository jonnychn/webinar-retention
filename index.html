Retention Data Generator with Chart Visualization

How to Use:
1. Copy your CSV data (which must include at least “Person” and “Time Duration” headers) directly into the text box below.
2. Click “Process CSV Data”.
3. The tool will calculate minute-by-minute retention (what percentage of people remain past each minute).
4. A table will display detailed results, and a chart will show retention over time.
5. You can download the processed CSV and the chart image using the provided download buttons.

Example CSV Data:
Person,Time Duration
John,15
Mary,10
Alex,20
...
